The Direction of Data

With each click of a mouse or tap of a finger, whether streaming a video or loading a page, data makes a physical journey through a global network of cables and cell towers.5 Although this travel seems invisible and detached, it actually accounts for a significant portion of our personal carbon footprint as humans.6

  • The Cost of Quality | Higher resolution video requires more work from the network, leading to higher electricity consumption per minute.6
