Defining Our Impact

To understand how the web affects our planet, it is important to grasp key concepts that shape what we now know about website usage and how and what it affects. These concepts shift our focus from the continuous and instantaneous availability of technology to continue to nurture and protect the planet we live on.

Concept Definition
Digital Awareness The practice of "digital sobriety," which involves monitoring personal data consumption to mitigate the environmental impact of Information and Communication Technologies (ICT) and reducing unnecessary data traffic.6
The Invisible Root System The physical infrastructure of the web, consisting of data centers that house IT operations while consuming billions of gallons of water for cooling and requiring vast amounts of electricity to function.2,5
Mindful Design An engineering approach that prioritizes energy efficiency by reducing data-heavy media and complex code, directly lowering the energy demand of transmission networks and the processing power required by end-user devices.3,5
Data Composting The systematic deletion of obsolete or redundant data to reduce the physical storage footprint and the continuous electricity required to power and cool the servers where that data resides.5
Digital Responsibility The transition toward sustainable digital practices to address the fact that data centers and transmission networks currently account for approximately 1% to 1.5% of global electricity use and require significant mineral resources, such as copper and lithium, for hardware production.4,5
